# Game asset comparison (local copies, updated 2026-11-07)

This is an inventory and compatibility assessment for the pinned **Z0MB1ES (on teh ph0ne) v1.0.0.0** XAP used by this port, the local `game/time-vicking-360` and `game/zombies-360` Xbox 360 Indie Game packages, and the installed PC game at `D:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\IMAGWZII`. The Xbox package names are local filenames; their `GameInfo.xml` titles identify **TIME VIKING!!!!!ANDSPACERAPTOR** and **I MAED A GAM3 W1TH Z0MB1ES!!!1**. `IMAGWZII` is a PC build of **I MAED**, not a package for all three phone games. These observations apply to these exact local copies, not every release of the games.

## Size and coverage

Sizes are binary MiB (1 MiB = 1,048,576 bytes). “Content” counts files below each build's `Content/` directory, including audio banks and companions but excluding executables, package metadata, and the phone's nine custom data files outside `Content/`. Package sizes and installed/extracted sizes are different measures; Xbox STFS and XAP containers add overhead or compression.

| Copy | Container / install | `Content/` files | `Content/` size | What it covers |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Phone XAP | 53.89 MiB XAP | 94 (91 XNB, 3 WMA) | 72.56 MiB | I MAED, Time Viking, and phone-only Endless, plus shared menus |
| `time-vicking-360` | 38.13 MiB STFS | 27 (23 XNB, 1 WMA, 1 XWB, 1 XSB, 1 XGS) | 37.52 MiB | Time Viking; also contains a small `gfx/zombie/` subset |
| `zombies-360` | 12.71 MiB STFS | 16 (11 XNB, 2 XWB, 2 XSB, 1 XGS) | 12.45 MiB | Original I MAED only |
| Steam `IMAGWZII` | 88.11 MiB installed directory | 20 (15 `.xnb`-named files, 2 XWB, 2 XSB, 1 XGS) | 82.62 MiB | PC I MAED only |

The phone's `Content/` consists of 27.15 MB XNB and 48.94 MB WMA (decimal MB). The Xbox I MAED content is 2.68 MB XNB and 10.36 MB XACT banks. PC I MAED is 42.35 MB `.xnb`-named files and 44.29 MB banks. Steam has a 1280×720 texture for each of the eight named background images; the phone's matching images are 640×360. Its `sprites` atlas is 1280×1280 versus 640×640 on the phone. That is twice the width and height, or four times the pixels, before accounting for content differences. The PC build also has `spritesPC`, `crowdcontrol_logo`, `omni_logo`, and `ska_logo` that are absent from both Xbox and phone I MAED asset sets. `fx/thresh.xnb` in the PC install does **not** begin with an XNB header, despite its extension, so it needs separate effect handling.

The phone Time Viking textures include 512×512 atlases (`city`, `grass`, `hearts`, `scrap`, `sprites`, `urban`, `viking`), 2048×2048 shared `gfx/maps` and `maps2`, three separate `moon` images, touch controls, and scene images. Most Xbox 360 XNB textures are LZX-compressed `XNBx` files, so their compressed sizes must not be read as a texture-quality ranking. I decompressed them with the local FNA LZX decoder and used the sibling `xnb_parse` reader to inspect dimensions, formats, and RGBA pixels.

## Decoded Xbox textures

The Xbox I MAED backgrounds and sprites have the **same dimensions as Steam** and twice the phone dimensions on each axis. The Xbox background textures use DXT1; Steam uses uncompressed XNB `Color`. The Xbox `sprites` atlas uses DXT5, while Steam uses `Color`. Visually, all eight Xbox/Steam background pairs show the same compositions, and their alpha-composited pixel mean absolute differences are only **1.2–2.6 levels out of 255** at native 1280×720. Xbox/Steam `sprites` has a 1.3-level visible-image difference despite a much larger raw-byte difference in transparent pixels. This supports shared artwork with different texture encoding, not byte identity. Steam also has its own additional PC art named above.

Phone I MAED backgrounds use the same subjects and layout at 640×360, but **the phone images have a dark vignette baked around the edges** that is absent from Xbox and Steam. After box-downsampling Xbox images to phone dimensions, their visible RGB differences range from **5.6 to 24.5 levels out of 255** across the eight backgrounds; the vignette contributes to those differences. The phone and Xbox sprite atlases have the same broad arrangement after 2× scaling, with a 1.4-level alpha-composited RGB difference. These are related assets, not interchangeable outputs of a simple format conversion.

| Texture group | Phone | Xbox 360 | Steam PC | Assessment for reuse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I MAED: eight backgrounds | 640×360 `Color`, baked vignette | 1280×720 DXT1 | 1280×720 `Color` | Same artwork/composition. PC and Xbox are visually close at the same size; preserving the phone look needs the edge treatment if either replaces its textures. |
| I MAED: `sprites` | 640×640 `Color` | 1280×1280 DXT5 | 1280×1280 `Color` | Same broad atlas layout after scaling; verify every source rectangle and effects before substituting. |
| Time Viking: common named atlases | Mostly 512×512 DXT5; `atmos`/`blue`/`gray` are 240×240 `Color`, `punk`/`punk2` are 336×320 `Color`, and `robot` is 585×384 `Color` | Corresponding atlases are 1024×1024, 480×480, 672×640, and 1170×768 `Color` | No supplied counterpart | Twelve of 13 matching named textures keep their art in corresponding positions at 2× scale. `grass` is the important exception: its icons move and change size. See the detailed audit below. |
| Shared `maps` and `maps2` | 2048×2048 DXT5 | 2048×2048 `Color` | No supplied counterpart | Same dimensions and visually almost identical layouts; only 0.67–0.69 levels of alpha-composited difference. These Xbox files offer a less compressed source, **not** a resolution increase. |
| Time Viking moon stages | Three 512×192 `Color` images: `moon1`–`moon3` | One 2048×2112 `Color` atlas: `moon` | No supplied counterpart | The same three stages appear in order, but each phone image is a centered crop from an Xbox atlas row at half size. Cropping and atlas mapping are required. |
| Time Viking Xbox-only images | No direct phone counterpart | `raptor` 932×588 DXT5; `controller` 40×40 DXT5; `covers` 1754×700 `Color` | No supplied counterpart | Additional Xbox art, not replacements for the phone's scene/touch assets. `covers` includes an Endless cover image; it is not an Endless gameplay asset set. |

The Xbox Time Viking package also contains a 1280×1280 `gfx/zombie/sprites` atlas, visually related to I MAED's sprites, but not its eight backgrounds, music bank, or Endless mode content. The I MAED Xbox package has no Time Viking atlases. All texture comparisons above use decoded pixels and inspected thumbnails; the differences are not subjective claims about how the full games render.

### Time Viking art match audit

I compared each phone texture with the same-named Xbox texture after box-downsampling the Xbox version to phone dimensions. For sprite sheets, **alpha overlap** measures whether visible pixels occupy the same locations (1.0 means complete overlap), and **luminance correlation** measures whether the visible artwork has the same structure (1.0 means complete correlation). These measures supplement direct inspection of side-by-side images; they do not prove exact sprite-coordinate compatibility.

| Art | Match assessment | Evidence and consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `atmos`, `blue`, `gray` | **Matches** | Same gradients/placement at 2× scale. Alpha overlap 0.995–1.000; luminance correlation approximately 1.000. |
| `city`, `hearts`, `punk`, `punk2`, `robot`, `scrap`, `sprites`, `urban`, `viking` | **Matches visually, with encoding/downscale differences** | Corresponding figures and elements occupy the same broad atlas positions. Across these nine sheets, alpha overlap is 0.906–1.000 and luminance correlation is 0.920–0.997. Their raw RGB values differ more in transparent pixels, which are invisible during normal compositing. Pixel-exact substitution is not established. |
| `grass` | **Partial match; atlas layout differs** | The grass strips and bare tree depict the same subjects. The phone places four large face icons down the right edge. The Xbox sheet places smaller faces nearer the center and includes an extra green raptor face. Alpha overlap falls to **0.685** and luminance correlation to **0.496** after the ordinary 2× downscale. Reusing this sheet needs specific source-rectangle remapping or repacking; a global scale factor would select the wrong icons. |
| Shared `maps` and `maps2` | **Matches closely** | Both sides are 2048×2048 with the same atlas layout. Luminance correlations are **0.9997** and **0.9996**, and alpha overlaps are **0.9970** and **0.9978**. The phone uses DXT5 while Xbox uses `Color`, explaining small pixel differences without an atlas move. |
| `moon1`–`moon3` versus Xbox `moon` | **Same stage artwork, differently cropped** | Xbox `moon` has three 2048×704 rows. Each phone stage matches a roughly centered **1024×384 crop** of the corresponding row downsampled 2×. Template matching found source crop starts near x=504–512, y=6–10 within each row; matched luminance correlations are **0.951–0.988** and alpha overlaps **0.984–0.995**. Reducing the full atlas row to 512×192 gives a visibly different framing. Split/crop it before use. |
| Xbox `raptor`, `controller`, `covers` | **Additional or repackaged art** | The phone has no same-named textures. `raptor` is additional character art, `controller` is Xbox UI art, and `covers` combines game covers (including a Viking character recognizable in the phone's separate scene art). None is a direct replacement for a phone texture. |

The practical result is that **most Time Viking artwork is the same**, often with a 2× source image, but `grass` and `moon` require explicit asset mapping. The phone also keeps separate scene images and touch graphics. An in-game rendering pass is still needed before claiming that every animation and source rectangle works with the Xbox set.

## Music and sound quality

The measured properties below are **codec/stream metadata**, not a listening test. XACT wave banks are containers: `ffprobe` could not open the original `.xwb` directly, so I read their entries with the sibling `xnb_parse` XWB parser. It identified one music entry in each I MAED bank. The exported PC WAV wrapper reports a misleading 971-second duration in `ffprobe`; the original wave-bank entry metadata reports **817.83 seconds**, consistent with the Xbox entry's **817.84 seconds** and phone `epicopus.wma`'s **817.95 seconds**. Use the wave-bank metadata for this comparison.

| Music source | Codec / channels / rate | Music bytes | Duration | Quality assessment |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Phone `epicopus.wma` (I MAED) | WMA v2, stereo, 44.1 kHz, 192 kb/s stream | 19,735,319 | 817.945 s | Lossy; the port converts it to Ogg for FNA playback. |
| Xbox I MAED `music.xwb` | XMA2, stereo, 44.1 kHz | 9,981,952 bank; 9,979,904 encoded music bytes | 817.842 s | Lossy Xbox codec; smaller size does not prove worse audible quality. |
| Steam I MAED `music.xwb` | Microsoft ADPCM, stereo, 44.1 kHz, 4-bit, approximately 419 kb/s encoded data | 42,831,872 bank; 42,828,736 encoded music bytes | 817.830 s | More bytes per second, but ADPCM and perceptual codecs are not directly comparable by bitrate. No objective or listening comparison was performed. |
| Phone `timeviking.wma` | WMA v2, stereo, 44.1 kHz, 192 kb/s stream | 21,900,735 | 907.667 s | Same nominal format, size, and duration as the Xbox copy, but file and decoded-audio SHA-256 differ; not byte-equivalent. |
| Xbox Time Viking `timeviking.wma` | WMA v2, stereo, 44.1 kHz, 192 kb/s stream | 21,900,735 | 907.667 s | No evidence that one encoding sounds better. |
| Phone `endless.wma` | WMA v2, stereo, 44.1 kHz, 192 kb/s stream | 7,306,547 | 302.045 s | No corresponding track in the two Xbox packages or Steam install. |

Phone music XNBs are small Song descriptors, not the music payload. The phone has 19 PCM SoundEffect XNBs; the Xbox I MAED bank has 31 XMA2 effects, the PC bank has 31 ADPCM effects, and Xbox Time Viking has 34 PCM effects. Those counts and codecs establish neither one-to-one cue identity nor audible superiority. This port's [audio preparation](audio-portability.md) keeps phone PCM effects and transcodes phone WMA to Ogg Vorbis; conversion cannot restore information absent from the WMA source.

| **ZOMBIES ENDLESS** (`gfx_2/zombie/` plus `endless.wma`) | No standalone Xbox or Steam counterpart in these local copies | **No complete counterpart.** The phone shares some zombie texture names with I MAED, but its Endless music and mode-specific game implementation are in the XAP. | Retain phone assets for this mode. Reusing PC background/sprite art would require a mode-specific visual and coordinate check; it cannot supply the missing Endless music or phone-mode content. |
| **Shared phone UI and data** (`gfx/`, achievements, custom art, `maps/data`, `scene/data`) | No complete equivalent in either standalone Xbox package or PC I MAED | Mostly phone-specific assembly assets. The XAP has 20 achievement textures, eight 100×100 custom textures, a SpriteFont, map/scene data, and touch controls. | Keep these from the XAP. The Xbox packages also have platform metadata/executables that do not replace phone menus or data. |

For this port, **the XAP remains the complete input of record**. The existing [content coverage](content-coverage.md) shows all 91 phone XNBs load under FNA after preparation, with five path aliases for scene art and Ogg companions for the three WMA songs. The current runtime loads the original phone asset names. A desktop upgrade path would need an explicit per-game asset map and checks for atlas coordinates, format loading, and visual alignment. Texture name overlap or greater PC resolution alone is insufficient to replace assets safely. Xbox Time Viking has no corresponding modern PC directory in the supplied set.

## Method and limits

- Inspected the exact local packages and install above. Extracted both STFS archives with `../xna-to-fna-asset-extractor/xbla-extract/stfs_extract.py` into ignored `private/asset-comparison/`, leaving `game/` untouched. Inventoried the already extracted XAP under `private/original-xap/`.
- Counted file lengths under `Content/`, compared same-name files using SHA-256, and inspected WMA with `ffprobe`. Parsed XACT banks with `xnb_parse.xact.xwb.XWB`; XMA2/ADPCM/PCM identifications come from their entry headers. The phone/Xbox Time Viking WMA decoded-audio hashes were checked with FFmpeg and differ.
- The sibling extractor inside `../z0mb1es-on-teh-fna/asset-extractor/` is a wrapper around the same `xna-360-and-wp7-asset-extractor` tool. Its bundled `xnb_parse` uses `XnaNative.dll` and requires 32-bit Windows Python for compressed XNBs. To complete this comparison under 64-bit Python, I invoked the local FNA `LzxDecoder` against the extracted Xbox XNBs, validated each decompressed length, then parsed and decoded their textures with `xnb_parse`. All inspected Xbox textures decoded successfully. The helper and extracted assets remain under ignored `private/asset-comparison/`.
- For numeric image comparison, I decoded Xbox, phone, and Steam textures to RGBA. Different-sized images were box-downsampled to phone size. Reported image differences are mean absolute RGB differences after alpha compositing on black (0–255 scale); fully transparent RGB bytes do not count. I also inspected contact sheets for layout and composition. These checks do not validate texture coordinates in the running port, visual effects applied by the games, or audible quality.
- For the detailed Time Viking audit, alpha overlap is intersection over union of pixels with alpha above 16; luminance correlation is Pearson correlation of alpha-composited grayscale pixels. Moon crop positions were found by normalized image template matching after 2× downsampling each Xbox atlas row. The reported crop origins are approximate pixel registrations, not a production crop specification.
- Keep extracted proprietary files in ignored `private/`. This report contains metadata and findings, not game assets.
